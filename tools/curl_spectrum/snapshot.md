## D — total damage of one cast (`max_hops = 8`)

What a cast IS: eight waves, seeded on a legal target (graph degree ≥ 4,
Cyclone's `min_degree`) with the caster on a neighbour, averaged over every
arrival edge. Multiples of the seed impact — 1.00 would be *the seed and
nothing else*.

Each cell is `territory / whole map`: the same eight waves confined to a
60-node holding — the ground `OwnerFilter` (Hostile-only) actually lets a
cast walk — and then over an all-hostile map, which is the ceiling. `Δ8` is
the whole map's last-wave growth over the wave before it.

## E — real casts: what the linear model cannot see

`SpellResolver` on the shipped `cyclone.tres` over the same terrain, with
`closing_gain` moved and the cycle crit stripped (it multiplies what a hop
LANDS, not what it forwards). Totals are multiples of the seed impact, so
they read against table D's column for the shipped coefficients. The
defender's nodes are given 1e7 HP: a kill would truncate the walk and
confound the walk's growth with the map's HP roll.
