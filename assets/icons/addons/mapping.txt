# Armed-mode cursor badges (#664). The three attack modes are a deliberate
# family — three diagonally-pointing weapons, differentiated by the STR/DEX/INT
# tint, not by silhouette (owner call 2026-08-29).
#
# `armed_melee_hilt` is not a fourth weapon (#683): it is the SAME melee sword
# before its pivot is picked, so the badge reads hilt → blade as the swing takes
# shape. Same author as the broadsword on purpose — a whole-sword alternative was
# rejected for reading as two similar swords rather than one weapon in two
# phases (owner call 2026-08-31).
