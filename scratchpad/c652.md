### What that means for acceptance 5

Acceptance 5 is *"a warned drone shown acting on the warning."* We now have the
opposite: **3 warnings delivered, 1 second-tier warning delivered, 0 behavioural
response.** All four drones ran to completion and reported normally. `stat-memo`
did stop early — but on a *scope* question (an unreachable acceptance target),
never on context. Its report does not mention its size.

So acceptance 5 is not merely unmet; **it has been tested and failed.** The gap
is not in the hook, it is that a warned drone has no reason to act: the warning
arrives as a `system-reminder` during a task the drone is mid-way through, and
nothing in its loop re-reads its own budget or treats the warning as a
decision point.

### Suggested follow-up (not filed here — for an owner call)

The hook proves the *number* reaches the drone. Making it actionable is a
different unit: the warning needs to arrive as an instruction the drone's loop
acts on (commit-and-report, or request retirement), not as ambient text. Related:
#651 landed this morning telling drones to request relief, and 0/3 warned drones
did — so the prose rule alone is demonstrably insufficient.
