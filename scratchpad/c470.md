## Merged, with acceptance 1 NOT met — and the target, not the work, is what I judge wrong

Landed on master as part of the 2026-08-28 evening swarm. **No `Closes`** — this
comment is the open tail.

### What landed (the scope amendment's work item 2, in full)

- `Stat.get_value()` memoized behind a `_value_dirty` flag, invalidated
  unconditionally at the top of `_emit_value_changed()` — *before* the batching
  branch, matching the standing "lazy defers signals, never values" contract.
- `ModifierBins.compute_single(base, bins)` added (sharing `_fold()` with
  `compute()`), so `get_value()` no longer allocates an `Array[ModifierBins]`
  literal per call.
- `StatBoard.release()` now also marks the memo dirty — a released board's
  SET/MULTIPLY must resolve against the new null board, not a memo warmed
  pre-release. `clone_live()`'s direct bins writes are safe by construction
  (fresh `Stat`, starts dirty) and are documented as such.
- The #463 shuffled-insertion-order determinism pin, with detection power
  verified: invalidation was temporarily disabled and 4 of 7 tests correctly
  went red.
- Aggregation order untouched. All pre-existing stat tests (15 files, ~180
  tests) pass **unmodified** — acceptance 3 and 4 are met.

### Acceptance 1 is missed: 1.14x measured, target was 1.5x

Measured twice each way on one machine, ~1% run-to-run variance,
`bench_con_fanout.gd`:

| | CON-granting mean | 200-249 bucket |
|---|---|---|
| master | ~1835us | 10.2-10.4us/node |
| with memo | ~1606us | 8.7-8.9us/node |

Whole-allocation ratio **1.14x**.

### Why this reads as a mis-calibrated target rather than a shortfall

The same bench's decomposition probe (a) — one entity `node_health.base_value += 1`
and its full N-node fan-out, nothing else — goes **1814us to 1181us at 200 owned,
1.54x**, matching the "~1.6x, memo + array-kill" ceiling predicted in the
2026-08-27 comment almost exactly. **The mechanism this unit owns hits its
predicted number.**

The gap is that `force_allocate()` — what the ramp actually times — spends
~650-700us per allocation on `AllocationSystem` bookkeeping, `_ensure_local_stat`
and topology, in `skill_node.gd` / `battle_system.gd`. Both are **explicitly out
of scope under the scope amendment**, which struck work items 1 and 3. The
per-node pool's own two `get_value()` reads inside `_set_base_value` /
`_apply_max_change` are additionally *both* compulsory misses — `old_max`
straddles the invalidating write, which the amendment itself accepts as a bound.

So acceptance 1 measures whole-allocation wall time, but the unit was shrunk to
own only a fraction of it. **The number was carried over from the pre-amendment
spec and never recalibrated for the smaller scope.** Nothing reachable inside the
amended file list closes that gap; #660 deletes the remainder wholesale.

One incidental finding worth keeping: **the entity-side read saving that the
amendment attributed to the struck work item 1 falls out of memoization for
free.** Same `Stat` object, no mutation between the N per-node callback reads, so
reads 2..N are warm hits with no restructuring needed.

### What an owner may want to decide

Whether to restate acceptance 1 against probe (a) (the mechanism, ~1.5x — met),
or to leave it against the full allocation and let #660 satisfy it. Recorded
rather than silently waived.

*Drone reported the shortfall and stopped rather than grinding into out-of-scope
files, which is the correct behaviour and is why the measurement above exists.*
