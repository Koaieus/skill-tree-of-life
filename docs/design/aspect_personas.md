# Aspect Personas — the lesser gods who talk

> Design doc, lore side. Each persona belongs to an aspect in
> [aspect_matrix.md](aspect_matrix.md). A persona needs a real aspect row
> there, and this doc never adds one. The matrix changes often until every
> aspect is in. This doc changes whenever a character idea turns up.
> Nothing here is scheduled work.

## Where they sit

Owner, 2026-09-30: attributes (STR DEX INT WIS PER CON) are the abstract,
Titan-class gods; aspects are the lesser gods who materialize, act and
talk. The cosmology lives in [lore.md](lore.md), *The pantheon*.

Owner, 2026-09-30: for a single-player story expansion (wayyyy later),
the aspects could be introduced one at a time, in order. Each debut is
where that persona's character gets to shine in the dialogues. They get
voices eventually, but the game has no audio yet, so that is later work.

## The personas

Owner's sketches, 2026-09-30, kept close to verbatim.

### Poison: Ivy / Oivy (`poison_aspect`)

Green-ish, radiant, cuddly yet insidious. Favourite encouragement: *"you
have got it in you! running through your veins"*.

The name: "Ivy" is the same plant pun as DC's Poison Ivy, and the owner
came up with it independently. "Oivy", said in a heavy Brit accent, is a
"u got a loicense for that mate" kind of joke. That's fine, but many
players won't get it. Owner idea: keep switching between *Oivy* and *Ivy*
in the text, depending on who is trying to pronounce the name and what
they actually say. Before there are voice lines this is only a spelling
game.

### Wither: Withou-Chu? (`wither_aspect`)

Gray tones, sad-ish. Possibly only quotes U2, leaning on "Wither
Withoutyou" before snapping out of it. Or maybe their name really is
**Withou-Chu**.

### Corruption (`corruption_aspect`)

A sleek, devilish business"man". Archetypal capitalist who loves
stacking and has a soft spot for deals, especially ones that cost morals.

### Curse: Cuss / Cussy (`curse_aspect`)

Owner, 2026-09-30: named **Cuss** or **Cussy**. There is an explicit or
veiled curse word in virtually every sentence, and bits of the dialogue
may be bleeped or blacked out. Cuss definitely holds grudges.

Owner, 2026-09-30, settled:
- **Serene, with crash-outs.** Calm by default, even the cursing sounds
  serene, and now and then Cuss flies off the handle for real. Agent
  suggestion for the trigger: a curse spills onto surviving neighbours
  when its host dies, so a crash-out could fire when the target of a
  grudge escapes by dying and the curse has to jump elsewhere.
- **Old Tongue on the page.** Cuss's curse words are written in symbols or
  runes and partly censored. The redaction hides actual casting, the same
  language as the Fairy's curse ("AAH FUUUCK", [lore.md](lore.md)). Weigh
  it against the Act 1 reveal before shipping it early.
- **Scoffs at positive words**, like "friendly" and "rainbows".
- **The grudge ledger.** Cuss keeps a running list of who wronged whom
  and brings up old entries. Owner: a fun basis for story progression
  with real choices.
- **Maybe family to the Fairy**, who also holds grudges, and so kin to the
  Lord of Edge (owner, 2026-09-30: "not too far fetched"; the Fairy most
  likely *is* the Lord). See [lore.md](lore.md), *The pantheon*.

- **Spikes** (`spike_aspect`): hedgehog-ish or something very pointy.
  Sharp, witty, loves to poke around, at people, at things.
- **Blunting** (`blunting_aspect`): maximal stoner. Yeah, take the edge
  off.
