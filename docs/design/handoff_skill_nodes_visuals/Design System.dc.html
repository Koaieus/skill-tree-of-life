Extended Stat Vocabulary — tint_color
Proposed tint_color values for every StatDef in docs/design/stat_system.md's canonical vocabulary — extending the six attribute hues rather than inventing a new palette. Rule: a derived/combat stat inherits the hue of the attribute that scales it (blade stats → STR red, ranged → DEX green, magic → INT blue); pools keep their established vital colors; turn-budget scalars keep the hues the HUD already committed to; a handful of stats with no attribute owner (aura, crit, rare/prestige) get new but chroma-matched hues. Hex + Godot Color() given for direct paste into the .tres files.
