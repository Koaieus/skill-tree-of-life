Edges & ports lab
First pass at the connective tissue between nodes. Ports — small sockets on the rim (a new ArcheNode ports prop, comma-separated angles) marking exactly where a graph edge attaches. Edges — neon beams drawn between them: bright flowing energy once both ends are allocated to the same entity, dim and static otherwise.
