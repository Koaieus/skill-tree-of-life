Gate — the one addon that lives on an edge
A 2-endpoint addon spanning an edge rather than a node — toggles it powered/depowered. Drawn as a diamond chip at the edge midpoint, not a corner badge, so its "this is an edge-thing" identity reads at a glance.
Allocate / Deallocate VFX
The live AllocationVFX needle-spike ("skill point from the heavens") already reads well — kept as-is, shown here as the disk's charge-in flash. The redesign targets the two flagged as so-so: voluntary deallocation now closes with an expanding ring at the empty socket (not just a fade), and forced deallocation pairs the particle shatter with a shockwave so the kill reads as an impact, not just a puff.
Allocate — needle spike (Lorentzian profile, SPIKE_NEEDLE_GAMMA) collapses into the disk as it charge-fills to the owner colour; ring brightens once.
Deallocate — disk drains and dims; a thin ring expands outward from the socket as the last of the colour leaves, marking the spot as empty again instead of just vanishing.
Signal Pulse — Modifiers Travelling to Core
The live coordinator (GlowingDot, PULSE_PER_HOP cadence) is a plain dot with a fading trail — legible but generic. Redesign: the comet now carries the source stat's colour (so a STR pulse reads red even mid-flight), each edge it crosses lights up in step (an edge is only ever gold when both ends share an owner — watch edge 1 flip live), and arrival at the core fires a real impact ring instead of nothing. Timing slowed ~3× here for legibility.
