# Q1 opinion — what number does Frozen's damage breaker read?

Researcher (Fable), 2026-11-11, for the owner. An opinion, not a ruling. Numbers below are the shipped ones: default board `armor` 0, `min_damage_taken` 5, `node_health` 100 (threshold 10% = 10), `crit_chance` 5%, `crit_multiplier` ×2; Dormant Cores armor 1/3/5, floor 5, HP 70/130/250 (thresholds 7/13/25); Bunker +5 armor / −5 floor; Pacifist core +3 armor; spell raw = (1 + ⌊√INT⌋) × power, so at INT 10 Spark 4, Rend 5, Lightning Bolt 12, Malediction 2 + 5 Curse (each +1 floor, landing before its own damage); a blade contact = `blade_damage` (2 + STR/20) × speed curve, so 2–3 slow, more at speed. `Mitigation.compute` is `max(floor, raw − armor)`; TRUE damage and the break's burst already bypass it.

## 1. Recommendation

**Shape (c), Frozen takes damage unmitigated** — with two amendments: every payload of the generation that is read at the ice lands unmitigated (the breaking hit's own landing, its 2 extra landings; the burst is TRUE already), and the defense readout greys armor and floor on a Frozen node. Confidence: medium (about 6 in 10). It is the only shape that keeps the owner's heavy-hit breaker as the owner stated it, raw ((b) keeps one too, mitigated), keeps ADR 0091 whole, is order-independent, and is a flag on the gathered entry to build. Its real price is a balance one the owner has to accept knowingly: Curse and Sunder are blind at the ice, and slow blade contacts land 2–3 there instead of the floor's 5.

## 2. The three shapes

**The arithmetic the owner asked for.** Landed under (c) minus landed today = `raw − max(floor, raw − armor)`. If the hit clears the floor (`raw − armor ≥ floor`) the attacker gains exactly `armor`; if it does not, the defender gains exactly `floor − raw`. So on the default board (armor 0) (c) never helps the attacker and helps the defender on every hit under 5: a slow blade contact (2–3), Spark below INT 16, Malediction's own 2, and anything landing on a Cursed node (5 stacks → floor 10: Spark lands 4, not 10). Negative armor is the same story, stronger: `effective_armor` returns armor ≤ 0 as is, so Rend's 5 Sunder (landing before its own 5 damage) makes `max(5, 5 − (−5))` = 10 land today and 5 under (c). Positive armor flips it: against a Large Core (armor 5) a Bolt lands 12 not 7; against a Bunkered node (armor 6, floor 0) 12 not 6. The owner's observation is right wherever armor is zero or below, and reverses wherever it is above.

### (a) Crit and blade only
- *Sees:* a 12 floater on a 100-HP Frozen node and no shatter; a crit toast or a heavy vertex, and the shatter. No number to contradict.
- *Favours:* the defender, strongly. The one generic counter to Frozen goes; with `crit_chance` 5% a spell without a crit condition breaks ice about one cast in twenty. Owned and unowned ice get one rule (a holder-less node already has no flat breaker). A cheap leaf freeze gets stronger, the direction ADR 0082's "Frozen as anti-reach only" alternative is held in reserve against.
- *Costs settled rules:* ADR 0091 §3 and §4 fall (nothing left to test alone); ADR 0081 §3's "any damage at or above a flat threshold" and the owner's 2026-11-08 *"a hit that deals X amount of % max health"*; ADR 0088 §4 shrinks to "a light vertex's crit still breaks". The Cold spell's Ice form (#1789) no longer clears the threshold on damage alone.
- *Build:* cheapest of the three to rule, a deletion.
- *Example:* Lightning Bolt 12, no crit, on a 100-HP Frozen node (threshold 10). Today: shatters, 3 payloads + burst. Under (a): lands 12, the ice holds, the chain ends. A ×2 crit (24) still breaks.

### (b) The arrival's own aspect riders land before the read
- *Sees:* the floater *is* the break number. Malediction's 5 Curse then Spark: floater 10 on a 10-threshold node, shatter. Bolt on Bunker ice: floater 6 against 7, holds.
- *Favours:* armored defenders (Bunker ice breaks by damage only to a crit or a 13+ raw hit; a glancing hit on a negative floor heals and can never break); Curse attackers (a Curse arrow visibly softens ice). The ice is hardened by whoever's armor the node carries: owned ice on an armored node is the defender's wall, and unowned ice on the victim's armored node is harder for the freezer to break too. ADR 0091's "armor never hardens ice" falls, and the spirit of the owner's 2026-11-08 *"a deep-frozen node would require more damage to shatter… doesn't sound good"* extends to armor.
- *Costs settled rules:* ADR 0091 §3 superseded; ADR 0082's `revisit-when` fires (an aspect rider changes reach) so Curse and Sunder land in stage one at a Frozen node; a stage-two ordering rule. Under 0091 §1 every arrival of a generation lands before the one read, so a sibling's Curse raises the floor this arrival is read at: §4's "tested alone" becomes alone on damage, shared on floor — fine, it matches the floater, but it is a new sentence, and *which* arrival takes the 2 extra landings now depends on sibling riders.
- *Build:* the dearest. Stage one must run the rider pipeline (resistance, Hex, block, crit fold) for the arrival's floor- and armor-moving riders before `HitPoints.whole` → `Mitigation.compute`, or dry-run it: either a second landing order at Frozen nodes only, or a duplicated fold. This is a bounded version of the payload→terrain loop the owner called spaghetti in ADR 0091's alternatives.
- *Example:* Bunker on a Small Core (armor 6, floor 0, HP 70, threshold 7), Bolt 12. Today: read 12 ≥ 7, shatters while the floater says 6. Under (b): floater 6, read 6, holds.

### (c) Frozen takes damage unmitigated
- *Sees:* thrown = shown = read. A 12 lands as 12 on a Frozen node whose unfrozen neighbour would show 7; the defense card still says armor 5 (hence the grey-out).
- *Favours:* the attacker wherever there is armor (every Dormant Core, Bunker, Pacifist, Serpent, procgen's universal `armor` rolls), by exactly the armor; the defender on the armor-less default board by the floor shortfall (slow blades, low-INT Spark, any Cursed node). Owned ice on an armored node becomes a trap against spells: a Bolt that breaks it lands 3 × 12 + burst where the unfrozen node takes 9 — the owner's *"it cuts too: it takes increased damage"* (ADR 0088 §5) taken further than it was said. Unowned ice lets the freezer hit through the victim's plate. Owned and unowned ice share one rule.
- *Costs settled rules:* none superseded; ADR 0091 §3–§4 stand. Costs elsewhere: both anti-armor families go dark at the ice — Curse's role (`status-effects.md`: *"answers armor: every hit lands again"*) and Sunder's (Rend's own hit drops from 10 to 5) — exactly where Frozen + Bunker is the stack they were for; the sub-zero-floor heal (Bunker, procgen −1..−3) is off at the ice; melee at the ice loses the floor's lift (2–3 lands, not 5), which rubs against ADR 0088/0090's "melee must stay viable against Frozen" — weight still breaks, the break's landings just shrink.
- *Build:* small, but not one line. Under ADR 0091 §1–§2 the shatter is read and the store spent in stage one, so by the time the breaking hit's damage lands the node is no longer Frozen: the rule keys on "Frozen at this generation's read", a flag on the gathered entry (the record's blast table), which the landing hands to `Mitigation` as the TRUE path it already has. The two AI pricers that call `Mitigation.compute` themselves (`entity/controller/ai_combat_scorer.gd:271`, `ai_blade_rollout.gd:623`) read the same flag or misprice ice; plus the readout tweak. Replay and the shadow world see the same number.
- *Example, the trap side by side:* a 100-HP node with armor 3 (a Pacifist core's), floor 5, threshold 10, Bolt 12, break count 2 (ADR 0090), burst TRUE and excess-sized so the same in every column. **Unfrozen:** one landing, `max(5, 12 − 3)` = **9**. **Frozen today** (0091 §3): read 12 ≥ 10, shatters, floater 9 — the owner's gaslight; 3 × 9 = **27 + burst**. **Frozen under (c):** read 12, lands 12, shatters; 3 × 12 = **36 + burst**. (Under (b): read 9 < 10, holds, one landing of **9**, no burst.) Cursed node (floor 10), Spark 4: today floater 10 and holds; under (c) floater 4 and holds.

## 3. A fourth shape, mine: show the thrown number at the ice

ADR 0091 §3 stands and the floater on a Frozen node shows the thrown beside the landed, `12 (9)` — the combined-reading pattern `ui/tooltip_fan/stat_value_row.gd` already uses for armor + floor. Zero rule cost, meets the criterion literally. Its cost is that it answers "the rule reads a number I never see" by showing two numbers, and the Curse case still reads backwards (floater `4 (10)`, no shatter at 10). The cheapest fix, not the best; I list it so it is seen and set aside, not missed.

## 4. Follow-ups my pick leaves the owner

1. **Scope of "unmitigated."** The breaking hit's 2 extra landings too (my default: yes — one node state, one rule for the generation), or only the arrival that was read? The burst is TRUE either way.
2. **Curse at the ice.** Accept it blind, or carve the floor back in (armor off, floor on)? The carve-out reopens the own-Curse drift and buys (b)'s ordering cost; I would not.
3. **A blade contact's amount at the breaker:** the `BladeDamageInstance` amount as stamped, `blade_damage × f(speed)`, whole-rounded, one contact one arrival. Under (c) a slow contact (2–3) never breaks by damage and lands 2–3; weight is its breaker (ADR 0088).
4. **A dedupe-merged landing** (owner: one arrival at its summed multiplier): unchanged — read and landed are both raw × the summed multiplier.
5. **Readout:** grey armor and floor on a Frozen node's defense card, or write "ice: lands as thrown"? Either; the card must not display a stat the formula skips (`stat-upkeep-and-combat.md`'s "displays right, computes wrong").
6. **Tuning watch:** if armored owned ice reads as a trap in play, the knob is the threshold or the break count (ADR 0090), not this rule; if Frozen + Bunker reads too strong instead, (b) is the shape to revive.

## 5. Rider pipeline: what sits between "thrown" and the floater

**The trace** (a spell hit; an arrow's or blade's is the same from step 2). (1) The producer stamps `hit.amount` — `DamageEffect._apply_spell` writes `state.damage`, i.e. `spell_damage × power` after the hop scale, the share and the load multiplier; this, with the crit multiplied in, is ADR 0091 §3's "raw amount as thrown". (2) `DamageInstance.land_on`: `rider_gated` — a damage *rider* with a gated `paired` hit or a missed `land_mask` lands 0; a primary hit never gates. (3) `resolve_amount(node)` — a `PERCENT_MAX` / `PERCENT_CURRENT` basis becomes HP of this node. (4) `CritRoll.apply` — `× crit_multiplier`; the crit itself was decided at the lead's first rider, a fact of the hit before anything lands. (5) `NodeCombat.take_damage`: `HitPoints.whole`, round up once. (6) `Mitigation.apply_to`: TRUE bypasses; else `max(floor, raw − effective_armor(armor, pierce))`. (7) A negative result is reclassified HEAL; `effective_amount` = the post-mitigation number (not post-soak). (8) The floater and the record read `effective_amount` — the contract on `attack/outcome/hit_instance.gd:179` (owner, 2026-08-21: the floater shows it, the health bar moves `hp_before → hp_after`, and on an overkill they differ on purpose); `attack_record.gd:374` stores it so a replayed floater shows the same number. (The two coordinators' `Σ|amount|` reads are camera-focus weights, not the floater.)

**Block, resistance, Hex and Greed are not on this path.** They live in `StatusInstance.land_on` (`host.blocks_status`, the stack fold) and change how many *stacks* a status rider lands; no step scales a `DamageInstance.amount` by a resistance. So a blocked or resisted hit never shows 0 or 6 and shatters at 12: the damage number has exactly two things between thrown and shown — basis-to-HP plus whole-rounding (steps 3 and 5), and mitigation (step 6). My (b) sentence named the status fold because under (b) the *floor* the read uses depends on how many Curse stacks actually land, which is where resistance and Hex bite; under (c) the floor is skipped, so they are irrelevant to the read.

**So (c) does not widen.** "Every step between thrown and landed" is already just mitigation once the read takes the resolved, whole-rounded amount — a build note, not a rule: the stage-one read runs `resolve_amount` and `HitPoints.whole` on the arrival's stamped amount (with crit and load in) before comparing to the threshold, so a 9.6 thrown shows 10 and reads 10, and a `PERCENT_MAX` hit reads as the HP it becomes on this node. Resistance and block stay live at the ice (they gate the hit's riders, as everywhere). Nothing here needs the read to move after a landing step, so it is still (c).

**Pick and confidence:** unchanged, (c), medium. If anything the trace firms it slightly: the thrown-vs-shown gap is one formula wide, which is what makes skipping it a one-flag rule rather than a second pipeline.
