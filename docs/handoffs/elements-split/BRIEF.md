- **Decisions**: the owner's sentence verbatim with its date, copied from `docs/design/elements.md` (cite the doc line range). Never paraphrase an owner call; never invent one. A pick the doc/handoff marks "pass pick"/"tentative" is written as "(pass pick, tentative)". The rulings in § Rulings below are "(owner, 2026-11-10)" or "(pass ruling, 2026-11-10)" as marked.
- **Knobs**: every number is an `@export` (with `@export_range` when bounded) on the resource/node that owns it, never a `const`; default from the handoff's knob list; tests assert ratios/invariants, never the literal default. Visual knobs name their editor feedback (setter redraw or a sandbox-host tab).

If it will not come out concisely, do not paper over it: write the file anyway and put `OPEN FORK:` lines at the very top naming the undecided thing and the options.

The doc specifies almost no looks: describe the required *read* (what the player must be able to tell), leave the art to the drone, and add this NOTES line where the unit has a visual of any size: "The look may be handed to a sub-agent working in this same worktree once the hook it draws from (signal, record field, scene stub) exists; the unit's drone stays the owner and reviewer of the result." (owner, 2026-11-10: *"the drone may offload that work to a sub-agent drone of their own … they work on the same thing anyway so they can coordinate closely and share a worktree"*).
