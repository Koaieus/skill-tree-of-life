Cast shapes, the reach half: what a Fire or Lightning shape in the forge's shape field does to a cast. A Lightning shape makes the seed land on every node within N hops; a Fire shape widens the seed into a disc. A shape acts on the cast's own seed, at once, and plants nothing. Child of hub #1716 (late unit); design home `docs/design/elements.md` § Shape and ride and § Landing resolution ("Every landing is a whole hit, and a go-off lands on its own node too"); ADR 0084 §1-§6. The shape *field* (`Infusion.shape_element`, `shape_points`, `shape_of(spell)`), its count check and the capacity are #{forge} and #{overqualified-casting}; the gather, the disc and the arc are #{resolver}, #{burning} and #{lightning}. This unit feeds a cast's shape into them. The Ice shape (the concentrate) is #{ice-cast}; the two share no decision.

## Acceptance spec

**Decisions** (owner, 2026-11-10; quotes from `docs/design/elements.md`)
- Shape and ride are independent and the caster picks each (ADR 0084 §1): *"sometimes you'd want to set Fire as the reach element, sometimes you'd want to leave Fire stacks (Heat), sometimes you'd want both, or neither, or some other combination, all this *besides* other riders you might want to add -- poison, curse, greed, whatever (which don't modify reach)"*. A shape reshapes the walk and plants nothing: *"their responsibility is reach and only reach"*. It acts on the walk, never on a hit, so a hit still carries no reach (ADR 0075) and no neighbour is ever chosen. Arrows and blades have no walk and so no shape.
- The shape table: Fire "widens the seed into a disc", Lightning "the seed gathers every node within N hops (level N)". Owner: *"that's exactly what i'd been trying to convey all this time"*.
- **Lightning talks in levels** (ADR 0084 §1): *"Lightning reach on a spell talks in *level*, where level N gathers up to N hops from target. level 1 -> ring 1; 2->ring 2. cost: {level} aka 1, +2 (3), +3 (6), etc"*. The seed lands on every node within N hops; level N costs 1 + 2 + … + N points, so the level is the largest N with N(N+1)/2 ≤ points (1 / 3 / 6 / 10 points give level 1 / 2 / 3 / 4; points 2, 4 and 5 buy nothing more). The walk's own `max_hops` is untouched.
- **Fire feeds the one disc radius** (ADR 0084 §2): r = A × (√power + B) × node radius with power = points × a multiplier knob: *"same shape, with multiplier. and the fit neednt be precise, the ballpark should feel right"*. The function, A = 2 and B = 1 are #{burning}'s (`disc_radius(power, node_radius)`); no copy of the formula or of A and B lands here.
- **A shape and a primed node of the same element add into one go-off** (ADR 0084 §3): *"such that effective powers of local + incoming stay invariant basically. N lightning shape hitting level M Shocked node creates N+M level arc (e.g. 1+1=2, 2 hops out)"*. One go-off, not two. **Fire adds the roots** (owner's pick over adding the powers and over excess counting as points): r = A × (√(points × multiplier) + √excess + B) × node radius. How many landings the seed takes from the combined go-off is a pass reading, not the owner's (Composition, "The feed").
- **The node pays its solo cost** (ADR 0084 §4; *"the solo cost"*): what its own go-off would have spent without the shape, the nodes within M hops for Shocked level M, the nodes in its own disc for Burning. The shape's part was paid in points. Pass pick, tentative: at least 1, so a small fire whose own disc reaches nobody still contributes its root and loses a stack. A node that is not primed pays nothing.
- **A shape and a go-off of another element are two reaches, concatenated** (ADR 0084 §5; owner picked *same as dual reach: concatenate*; the doubt behind it: *"its close nbs might get hit twice (once by fire once by lghtng)"*). The seed node takes the seed in full; ADR 0084 §5's "the seed node takes the seed once" is changed by the whole-hit call (next Decision): the other element's go-off is a channel, so the seed takes 1 + 1. A node inside both reaches takes one copy per reach, 2. #{resolver}'s one dedupe switch (`dedupe_dual_reach`) covers it; no second switch.
- **Every landing is a whole hit, and a cast's shape alone is not a channel for the seed** (owner, 2026-11-10, night; `docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, paragraph "Every landing is a whole hit, and a go-off lands on its own node too"; no ADR carries it yet, and it changes ADR 0082's share default, now 1, and ADR 0084 §5's "the seed node takes the seed once"). The owner, picking share 1 with a centre landing over share ½ or share 1 with the centre excluded: *"the hit hits *and* the blast hits, and the arc hits, and share 1 means no rounding for rider stacks"*. The hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it; a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2; terrain riders (Heat / Cold / Charge) land once per node per hit, so the extra landings carry damage and aspect riders only. "No other node reached, no go-off" stands: the centre landing happens only when the go-off does, and the centre copy is #{resolver}'s. On a cast's shape (owner: *"the shape for spells hmmm i think that one shouldn't count"*): a Fire- or Lightning-shaped cast on an unprimed node lands once on its seed, and the extra landing comes only from a primed node going off. A shape with another element's go-off: the seed takes 1 + 1 (the go-off's channel), a node inside both reaches takes 2.
- **A landing a shape adds carries what a go-off copy carries** (ADR 0084 §6): *"same as what would happen if the cast had no reach but the node was Burning/Shocked"*. That is the go-off's share of the seed's damage and riders (share 1 by default since the whole-hit call), the seed's crit, and it is a copy to a Frozen node (absorbed under the break threshold, #{ice}). The seed itself lands in full. Terrain riders are among them, so a Fire shape with Heat riders lays Heat at share over its whole disc in one cast, once per node.
- Riders first (ADR 0083 §1): the shape is read at the seed's gather head after the hit's element riders land, with the node's primed state. The owner's walkthrough: a Fire 2 shape with a +1 Charge rider on a node one short of Shocked lands the Charge, the node is Shocked, then *"the total reach is determined, gathering all AoE targets (Fire) and gathering all Hops targets (Lightning) by combining present state (Shocked / Burning) with incoming shape (Lghtn / Fire / ...) where possible"*: a disc from the spell plus 0 from the node, an arc of level 1 from the node plus 0 from the spell, every node in either taking the payload at share, the +1 Charge included. Under the whole-hit call the seed takes 2 (the seed and the arc's centre copy; the disc adds none), a node in both reaches 2, a node in one 1, and the Charge rider lands once per node.
- A shape's reach is shape-blind like every go-off (ADR 0084 §7, #{resolver}): every node inside it, allocated or not; the hit's mask decides who the copies hurt.

**Knobs** (tentative defaults, easy to change; tests read the knob, never the literal)
- Fire's `Element` def `.shape_power_per_point` = 5.0 (`@export_range(0.5, 20.0, 0.25)`): blast power per Fire shape point. **Open (ADR 0084 revisit-when): 5 is a ballpark, not a fit.** At A = 2, B = 1 it gives about 4.1 / 8.9 / 15.6 / 23.4 nodes at 1 / 3 / 6 / 10 points on 150 px spacing, against Lightning's 3 / 8 / 15 / 23 (`tools/disc_reach.py --a 2 --b 1 --per-point 5 --fit-ladder`). Editor feedback: the blast sandbox tab.
- Solo-cost floor = 1 (`@export_range(0, 3)` beside the disc `GoOff`'s knobs, name the drone's). **Open: the pass pick "at least 1".** 0 turns it off.
- Lightning's level cost has no knob: it is the triangular rule above, one pure function (written call: the owner stated a rule, not a table).
- The shares are not new: a shape-added landing uses the element's own `GoOff.share` (#{resolver}; default 1 since the whole-hit call, so the `ceil` per rider is idle at the default). Tests read the knob.

**Composition**
- **The read.** A cast's shape is `Infusion.shape_of(spell)` with `shape_points` (#{forge}). This unit acts when it answers Fire or Lightning; Ice is #{ice-cast}'s and anything else is no shape. No v1 spell authors a Fire or Lightning shape default, so the points are the forged `shape_points` (an authored baseline is parked, NOTES).
- **The feed.** At a spell cast's seed landing (each wave-0 seed; pass reading, tentative, for a multi-seed cast), `SpellResolver` hands #{resolver}'s gather the cast's shape: element and points. Later waves' landings carry none. At that head's read step, after admit and land, the gather routes the shape one of two ways, and gains no other shape-specific branch:
  - *The node is primed in the shape's element* (Burning for Fire, Shocked for Lightning): the shape is an extra input to that band's own `GoOff` call (the instance #{burning} / #{lightning} put on the band), which answers one combined go-off (ADR 0084 §3) at the node's solo cost (ADR 0084 §4). It is that band's go-off, so when it goes off it lands the band's centre copy like any go-off (#{resolver}). Pass reading, tentative, not the owner's (the doc's own pass reading, last sentences of "Every landing is a whole hit"): the combined go-off is that node's one channel, so the seed takes 2, not 3. The floor case (a node whose solo go-off reaches nobody, Acceptance 6) reads the same, since the combined go-off goes off.
  - *Otherwise* (an unprimed node, or one primed only in another element): the shape's reach is the same element's `GoOff` answered with the shape alone (level N with M = 0; a disc of `shape_disc_radius(points, 0, R)`), and the gather appends that answer as a list through #{resolver}'s any-producer step: a reach with no band behind it, no spend, no own damage and no centre copy. The shape is not a channel (owner, Decisions), so the seed takes 1 plus one centre copy per band of another element that goes off.
  The shape adds one reach list at most; every other primed band still fires its own. The lists are concatenated under #{resolver}'s `dedupe_dual_reach`.
- **Lightning.** #{lightning}'s arc entry point takes a level. This unit passes `level_for_points(points)`; on a Shocked node the arc returns the reach of level N + M and the spend for level M alone. On a node not Shocked M = 0, and the answer (the reach of level N, spend 0) is used as a list, not as a go-off (the feed above).
- **Fire.** This unit adds the combined radius beside #{burning}'s function, over the same A and B: `shape_disc_radius(points, excess, node_radius)` = A × (√(points × `shape_power_per_point`) + √excess + B) × node radius, excess 0 when the node is not Burning. With points = 0 it must equal `disc_radius(excess, node_radius)`. The disc `GoOff` gets the same extra input as the arc: on a Burning node it reaches by the combined radius and returns the solo spend (the count inside `disc_radius(excess, …)`, floored at the knob when the combined disc reaches someone). On a node not Burning its answer is a list, as above. Node radius is the seed node's (pass reading, tentative; it is what the node's own go-off reads).
- **The spend.** The gather commits the spend the go-off returns and never counts the reached nodes (#{resolver} already says so). A shape's list spends nothing. A combined go-off that reaches no node does not go off, spends nothing and lands no centre copy (the element law; "No other node reached, no go-off").
- **The copies** are ordinary gather entries: share (default 1), parent = the seed, `paired` to the head so they carry its crit; `Admits` answers them as copies; chains, the once-per-node set (terrain riders once per node per hit) and Lightning's Charge transfer (which moves only the stacks the node spent) are unchanged. The disc's own "little damage" (`damage_per_power × power`) reads the node's excess only, so a shape's list adds none and a combined disc adds the node's own (pass reading, tentative; whether the centre copy also takes it is #{resolver}'s).
- **Frozen seed** (pass reading, tentative, not the owner's; decided nowhere): #{pairs}' mute drops a Frozen node's own Burning and Shocked go-offs; the shape is the cast's, not the node's, so it is not muted. Under the mute the node counts as not primed for the feed: the shape runs alone as a list (M = 0, excess 0), the trapped stacks pay nothing, no centre copy lands, and the seed takes 1. Its landings on Frozen neighbours are copies and are absorbed. When the hit breaks the ice, the released go-off is the node's, and a shape of its element adds into it as on any primed node.
- **The record.** The shape's reach is marked in the attack record's blast table like any go-off, through #{resolver}'s mark seat. A list with no band behind it names the `GoOff` instance that answered it (tentative), so the existing disc and arc looks play at the shaped size and in the shape element's look.
- **The look.** #{forge-ui}'s shape line (element and points only) gains the effect, from the pure functions here: Lightning "level N: every node within N hops" and the points to the next level; Fire the disc radius in node radii. #{ice-cast} adds the Ice line on the same files.
- **The range preview.** #679's aim-time preview (`MagicAttackPlan`: `get_range_visual`, `get_node_role`) resolves the cast on a shadow world through `SpellResolver.resolve` and reads the outcome's timeline, while the gather runs at land time in #{applier}'s drain, so the preview never sees a shape's landings. It draws the shaped seed from this unit's pure functions instead: a Fire shape adds one `RangeVisual.Ring` at the preview target, radius `shape_disc_radius(points, 0, R)`; a Lightning shape paints every seen node within `level_for_points(points)` hops of the target `PROPAGATION` (`nodes_within` on the graph's mirror, shape-blind), fog-gated like today's preview. An aimed cast draws one per crossed seed (the multi-seed reading). Pass reading, tentative, not the owner's: the preview shows the shape's own reach only, never its add-up with a primed node (the riders that tip a node land only when the hit does) and never a node's own go-off.
