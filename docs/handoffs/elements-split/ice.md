## Acceptance spec

**Decisions** (owner, dated as quoted; `docs/design/elements.md` line ranges)
- Frozen on a hit: "a copy under the break threshold is absorbed whole (no damage, no riders), a path ends (0D); a direct hit lands (owner, 2026-11-09, picked *absorbed* over *lands, then stops*)" (:77; ADR 0082 §3, which stands). Frozen reads at two points (owner, 2026-11-10, picked *admit on arrival, the rest after riders*; ADR 0083 §2): the copy's absorption is checked against the node as it arrives, an absorbed copy lands no riders; the path-end and the break check read after the hit's element riders land. Only a direct Cold hit deepens a freeze; a Cold copy onto a Frozen node lands nothing (:679-681).
- The blade: *"We want to pop just the two vtx. Not too dramatic, and remember Ice is 0D. Point like. Single target. So no splash or anything just a fragile snap"* (owner, 2026-11-08, :176-184). First contact pops that one vertex; it breaks the ice only if the vertex is at least as heavy as the node: *"melee blade nodes shatter frozen nodes they hit *only if their radius is equal or larger than the node they hit* (specifically: if they have more weight; ignoring temporary modifications by e.g. passing over Cold (but not frozen) nodes)"* (owner, 2026-11-10, § A blade on Frozen). Weight = `(radius / 32)²`, the vertex's from its source radius × Bunker's `blade_mass` (owner picked *Bunker counts*), the node's from `SkillNode.radius`; a tie breaks; nothing per-contact (Cold drag) enters. A lighter vertex still pops and the ice holds (owner picked *snaps, ice holds*; fallback *deflects off the ice* *"if it turns out to nerf melee too much"* — not built here).
- The burst: *"frozen side: effect removed at %maxhp propto the amount removed"* (owner, 2026-11-08, :185-194): the shatter removes everything above T, overflow included, and deals excess × a knob fraction × max HP to the frozen node's holder as TRUE damage through the shared burst path corruption uses, nothing if unallocated. *"drop crit for now"* (owner, 2026-11-08).
- The breakers (owner, 2026-11-08, :196-207): a blade vertex's touch (gated by weight, above; only the touch is gated — owner picked, 2026-11-10 — so a light vertex's contact that crits or would deal threshold damage still breaks), any crit hit landing on the node, any damage at or above a flat threshold — *"a flat amount not scaling with excess …"*. The threshold applies to direct hits and arriving copies alike; a copy that breaks through also lands. Heat and decay end Frozen without a shatter. No re-evaluation: a hit's crit and damage are its own facts, fixed before it lands (crit conditions still read the node as it was before the hit, ADR 0083 §4); the Frozen band the breakers and the path-end read is the one after the hit's riders land (ADR 0083 §2), so a Heat hit that thaws the node passes through without a shatter and a hit that freezes it ends its own walk there.
- Every landing is a whole hit (owner, 2026-11-10, night: *"the hit hits *and* the blast hits, and the arc hits, and share 1 means no rounding for rider stacks"*; `docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, paragraph "Every landing is a whole hit, and a go-off lands on its own node too"; no ADR carries it yet — it moves ADR 0082's share default to 1 and changes ADR 0084 §5's "the seed node takes the seed once"). The hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it; a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2; terrain riders land once per node per hit, so the extra landings carry damage and aspect riders only. At a Frozen node every landing but the direct hit is a copy, answered on its own: a bystander both reaches touch takes two copies, and a centre copy (a go-off's landing on its own node, which meets ice only when a break releases a trapped go-off, #{pairs}) is a copy too. Each is admitted or absorbed by its own crit and damage facts against the node as its generation found it, so two under-threshold copies never add up to a break (a hit's crit and damage are its own facts). (tentative, not the owner's) A sibling copy's break in the same generation does not admit an under-threshold copy, since the admit step runs before any of that generation's riders land or breaks are read; and the node breaks at most once per hit, since the break check sits at the post-rider read, which runs once per node per hit, so two breaking copies give one shatter (as a primed node's own go-off fires once per hit). (tentative, not the owner's) Frozen is no channel: it reaches no other node, so a Frozen hit node takes its direct hit once, and a break adds the burst, not a landing. Owner on the doubling: *"damage doubles or triples, neat. Frozen should match that but we can tune it to do so i guess. and crits can stack on top of that"* — that tuning is open (NOTES).
- Deep freeze: *"deep-freeze stacks dealing bonus damage on breaking ice and get removed along with it"* (owner, 2026-11-06, :918-923).
- Travel verb: Frozen, 0D — "a path **ends**: hops, chains and arcs stop here" (owner, 2026-11-06, :884-891).
- A vertex copies its source's primed state: *"copy frozen node -> frozen blade node (detrimental so far, makes blade node fragile, but maybe we do add a damage increase to even it out?)"* (owner, 2026-11-09, :425-433). The Frozen vertex's snap is a concentrate (owner, 2026-11-09, :441-455): one contact hit, then the vertex pops; the bonus is an overlay on that vertex's `blade_damage` read at fill (*"bonus or multiplier riding the pipeline (see `overlay`)"*), scaled by Cold excess at fill.
- A blade's element is *"an addon per element"* (owner, 2026-11-09, :418-424): the Cold addon grants the rider and nothing else.
- Spike pop: *"The existing one just pops the one, docs may be stale"* (owner, 2026-11-08, :1206-1209) — settle the header against the code here.
- (pass reading, tentative) A spell walk's "path ends" reads the band after the arriving hit's riders land (ADR 0083 §2), before a break's spend settles; so an arrival that shatters the ice still ends the walk there, and one that thaws it does not.
- (tentative, not the owner's) A Frozen node with no holder: the doc sets the flat breaker at "10% of the holder's max HP" and names a holder-less case only for the burst ("nothing if unallocated"). Read: with no holder the flat breaker is void, so on an unallocated Frozen node a copy is absorbed unless it crits, and the ice breaks only by a crit or a blade vertex's touch (the spend lands, the burst is nothing). Heat and decay still end it.

**Knobs** (tentative defaults — easy to change; owners are the Ice `Element` def and its Frozen band as #{defs} shaped them)
- Frozen band `break_fraction_max_hp` = 0.10, `@export_range(0.0, 1.0, 0.01)` — the flat breaker, a fraction of the holder's max HP; void on a node with no holder (Decisions, tentative).
- Frozen band `shatter_fraction_per_stack` = 0.01, `@export_range(0.0, 0.2, 0.001, "or_greater")` — burst % max HP per excess stack.
- Frozen band `shatter_spend_fraction` = 1.0, `@export_range(0.0, 1.0, 0.05)` — share of the excess a shatter removes.
- Frozen band `vertex_bonus_per_excess` = 0.10, `@export_range(0.0, 1.0, 0.01, "or_greater")` — the Frozen vertex's multiplier, no base term.
- Cold addon innate load per vertex — the shape and default are #{blade-elements}'s per-element addon; Ice authors its value (default 1).
- Visual knobs (shatter shard count, crust tint) — setter redraw on the `@tool` visual, or the sandbox-host blade tab.
- No share here: a copy's share is #{resolver}'s `GoOff.share`, default 1 (the whole hit). The Frozen knobs above keep their tentative defaults; tuning the shatter to match the whole-hit doubling is open (NOTES).
Tests assert ratios (a deep freeze bursts more than a shallow one; burst ∝ excess), never these literals.

**Composition**
- The Ice `Element` def (#{defs}) carries the Frozen band; this unit adds the Frozen subclass of the `Admits` base #{defs} created in `elements/` and implements its answers (land / absorb / land + break). #{resolver} owns both seams that ask it, and where a walk ends on the answer (ADR 0083 §2). (1) **The admit step**, before the generation's riders land: an arrival is asked against the node as it arrives, with its crit and damage facts; Ice answers land or absorb. Absorb is for a copy only, under the threshold with no crit: no riders, no stage-two entry, no onward reach. A direct hit always lands. (2) **The post-rider read**, after the hit's element riders land, where the path-end and the break check are asked against the resulting band and the hit's crit, damage and (for a blade contact) weight facts: Ice answers whether the path ends here and, on a break (land + break), the spend and the burst. Crit conditions elsewhere still read the pre-hit node (0083 §4). Ice owns no gather code and no terrain write.
- A break's spend (the excess read at seam (2) × `shatter_spend_fraction`) is a number in Ice's second answer, which #{resolver}'s gather commits with the beat's go-off spends through #{flow}'s shared settle routine, the one terrain writer (#{terrain}). The burst amount (that excess × `shatter_fraction_per_stack` × max HP) rides the same answer and lands on the node in stage two through `DotTick.mint`, the path `CorruptionStatus._on_burst` uses, so "a burst lands" stays one implementation (tentative, not the owner's: the reading #{resolver} carries). The stage-two seat that lands an answer's burst is #{resolver}'s; if none exists at dispatch, stop and say so. Unallocated node: spend yes, burst nothing.
- Spell walk: Ice's second answer says the path ends at a node whose band is Frozen after the arriving hit's riders land; an absorbed copy lands none and makes no entry to walk on from. Ending the walk on that answer is #{resolver}'s seam (its expansion step, where `hops_remaining <= 0` ends a walk today, `spell_resolver.gd:383`); this unit writes no walk code.
- Whole hit at a Frozen node (Decisions): every landing but the direct hit reaches seam (1) as a copy, each of a bystander's entries and a centre copy alike, and is answered on its own. Ice adds no landing of its own: the Frozen band has no `GoOff`. A Frozen + Shocked node's mute and release, and the released go-off's centre copy, are #{pairs}'.
- Blade on a Frozen node: `BladePopResolver.LiveGate.admit` gains a Frozen check that runs *before* its unallocated (`blade_pop_resolver.gd:280`) and own-node (:289) early returns — terrain is side-less (:896-898), so your own and unowned Frozen nodes snap a vertex too — and pops the vertex through the existing `_kill` (no drain check). The gate runs on a shadow world and writes no terrain. The refused contact still runs stage one: it reaches #{resolver}'s gather as a direct hit (seam (1) lands it, as it lands any direct hit), and Ice's answer at seam (2) fires the blade-touch breaker (spend + burst) when the vertex's weight (its build-time mass `(radius / 32)²` × Bunker's `blade_mass` — not `1 / inv_masses`, which is 0 for the pivot and dead vertices; the pivot keeps its exemption, #{blade-elements}) is ≥ the node's `(radius / 32)²`, and otherwise still checks the contact's crit and damage against the other two breakers. The weight is a fact the contact carries into the gather, read from the build-time mass, never a per-contact one (tentative, not the owner's: seam (2) reads it from #{blade-elements}' go-off-source argument, the contacting vertex's `BladeState` + `particle_idx`; if a refused contact carries no such argument, say so in the report). Only its stage-two payload (damage, other riders) is dropped. Today a refused contact returns from `BladeDamageInstance.land_on` before anything lands (:29-33), so the drone threads the refused contact through #{resolver}'s gather, not around it. The pop itself stays recorded as today (`popped_vertex`, emitted by `OutcomeApplier.land_one`). If the hit scan emits no contact event for an unallocated node, say so in the report and keep the check for allocated nodes of either side.
- Frozen vertex: #{blade-elements}' copy-on-fill gives the vertex its primed copy, and it ships only the one-shot seat (the flag and its branch in `LiveGate.admit`, which lands a flagged vertex's first contact and then pops it). Ice (a) sets the one-shot flag at fill on a vertex whose copy is Frozen, so that seat pops it after its first contact hit lands (the hit lands, unlike a spike pop), and (b) adds the overlay on `blade_damage` at fill through the `overlays_for` hook, scaled by the copied excess. The source node is never touched.
- Cold addon: data on #{blade-elements}'s per-element addon base (rider only, budget by the `ice` count); no Ice-specific code.
- Dies: nothing. Frostbite (#1444) is not here: its own issue, blocked by this one.
