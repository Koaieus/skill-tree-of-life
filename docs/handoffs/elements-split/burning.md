## Acceptance spec

**Decisions**
- The blast gap closes on the spine (owner, 2026-11-10: *"option 1 but spare no drones, split if needed"*): Burning's disc lands before the retag; go-off zero's disc keeps riding the Explosive `Aspect` beside it until #{catalyst} deletes it. Master always has a blast.
- *Burning*, a hit on it: "explodes: a disc, power = the excess, a little damage ∝ power, the triggering hit's payload copied over the disc" (`docs/design/elements.md:73-75`).
- Spend: "Burning 1 per node its disc covers" (owner, 2026-11-08: *"at some explosion size, fire would hit basically "all its nbs" like equalling a shock to each nb"*); a disc that reaches no other node does not go off (*"possibly extend this rule to make it cost 0 if it hits nobody?"*, *"there should be at least 1 stack disappearing, possibly more"*); the spend digs under T (*"would decharge more rapidly"*) (`:79-93`).
- Riders first (ADR 0083 §1; owner, 2026-11-10, replacing the 2026-11-09 pre-hit read): the hit's Heat, Cold and Charge riders land, then primed-ness and power are both read from the result, so the hit that tips Heat over T sets the node off itself. Power = the excess after the land (`:93-102`).
- Size: r = A × (√power + B) × node radius, A = 2, B = 1, power = the excess (ADR 0084 §2; owner, 2026-11-10: *"`r(p) = A*(sqrt(p) + B) * node_radius` … edge hops have no min distance. euclidean has. so this might help?"*), replacing the 2026-11-08 √power × node radius and its `radius_per_node_radius` 1.0 (`:109-125`). With node radius R: power 1 gives r = 4R, power 4 gives 6R, power 9 gives 8R. On 150 px spacing the probe (`tools/disc_reach.py`) reaches 0.7 / 2.1 / 3.0 / 3.6 other nodes at power 1 / 2 / 3 / 4.
- **One radius function, three readers** (ADR 0084 §2). This unit owns it: one pure function `disc_radius(power, node_radius)` reading the knobs A and B, in one place. Its readers are Burning's go-off disc (here), Burning's light source ({fire}) and the Fire shape on a cast ({cast-shapes}, which feeds it points × its multiplier, and adds roots for a primed node, ADR 0084 §3). No reader copies the formula or holds its own A and B.
- Go-off zero's Explosive disc keeps 3 × √power × node radius (`reach_per_radius` 3.0, #{go-off-zero}) until #{catalyst} deletes it; it does not read the new function.
- The disc is the `GoOff` disc subclass on the `Element` def (owner, 2026-11-09: *"i think it should sit on Element def, going off is just about their thing"*), "go-off zero, then Burning unchanged with another power source" (`:734-747`).
- **Burning is what ignites** (owner, 2026-11-05: *"Burning ignites"*, picked over "any Heat ignites") (`:937-940`).
- Copies carry everything (owner, 2026-11-08: *"everything rides yeah, LET IT RIDE! can change that later if it becomes too much"*) (`:105-108`), so a Heat rider on a copy chains.
- Dual reach (owner, 2026-11-10: *"riders twice, payload twice. charged burning nodes sounds like a dangerous concept"*): a node holding Explosive charges and Burning sets off both discs, and a node inside both gets two entries, each landing everything at its share. The reaches are concatenated, with one dedupe switch at the gather step (ADR 0084 §5; it also covers a cast's shape against another element's go-off, built by {cast-shapes}). Each go-off fires once per hit (owner, 2026-11-10), through the once-per-node set. The disc takes every node inside it, allocated or not, at every size (ADR 0084 §7; owner, 2026-11-10: *"so far just keep it shape blind always"*); allocated-only at level 1 is a parked backup, not built. Under the whole-hit rule below, the hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it.
- **Every landing is a whole hit, and Burning's disc lands on its own node too** (owner, 2026-11-10, night, picked over share ½ with the centre excluded and over share 1 with the centre excluded: *"the hit hits *and* the blast hits, and the arc hits, and share 1 means no rounding for rider stacks"*; `docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, paragraph "Every landing is a whole hit, and a go-off lands on its own node too"; no ADR carries it yet). The share's default is 1, so a copy is the whole payload and the round-up per rider is idle. When the disc goes off it lands one more payload on the node it goes off from: the hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it, a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2. Terrain riders still land once per node per hit (stage one's cap), so the extra landing carries damage and aspect riders only: the hit's Heat lands once and a fire does not feed itself. "No other node reached, no go-off" stands: the centre landing happens only when the disc goes off, and it costs no Heat (the spend counts the other nodes reached). This changes ADR 0082's share default (was 0.5) and ADR 0084 §5's "the seed node takes the seed once". The centre entry is made by #{resolver}'s gather, not by the disc.
- The node pays its solo cost (ADR 0084 §4): Burning's own go-off spends 1 Heat per node its disc covers, exactly as above. When a Fire shape later combines with it, the node still pays that same solo cost for the combined go-off; the pass pick (tentative: at least 1) and the combination are {cast-shapes}'s, not built here.
- A ride row plants its named status: Heat rides "exactly as Poison rides" (the doc's rule, `:346-360`; the owner's 2026-11-04 line behind it is at `:354-357`).
- **This unit owns rekeying `Infusion.load_of` / `use_of`** so that a row named by element resolves its status (pass ruling: it is the first unit that needs it; #{defs} adds only the `plants` field, and #{catalyst} relies on this). The doc's row-keys item: a row names the status it plants (owner, #1660, 2026-10-18: *"B2: agreed"*), defaulting to its concept's only status; the forge budget stays keyed by concept, and everything else reads the rows instead of a concept's first row (pass pick; `docs/design/elements.md` § Open, adversarial review item (a)). For an element, "its concept's only status" is its one terrain stack (Heat for Fire): a state never rides, and the movers are empty in v1.

**Composition**
- Fire's `Element` def (#{defs}) owns Heat and its ordered bands. This unit authors the Burning band's `go_off` as a second instance of #{go-off-zero}'s disc subclass. Its power source is the band excess (Heat over T, read from #{terrain}'s signed `t` after the land step), not go-off zero's charge formula. If the disc subclass hard-codes go-off zero's power, the power read becomes the subclass's one override point. No second disc class.
- The radius function (Decisions) lives where #{fire}'s light and {cast-shapes}'s Fire shape can call it without importing the disc: a static helper beside the `Element` def's knobs, or on the disc subclass if the other two reach it through the def. Its seam is `disc_radius(power, node_radius) -> float`; the disc passes the band excess, the light passes Fire's light power, the shape passes points × multiplier (or the combined roots, ADR 0084 §3, which is a second function over the same A and B).
- #{resolver}'s gather asks the band to *go off*, through the `GoOff` contract #{resolver} states; the disc answers its four returns, in the contract's terms: the spend (1 Heat per node reached, a return of its own that the gather commits as returned), the nodes reached (Euclidean, through the spatial index go-off zero uses, the centre excluded), the share (`share`, default 1) and the own damage (`damage_per_power × power`, the "little damage ∝ power"). The gather owns chains, the once-per-node set, the one-snapshot settle and the whole-hit centre landing: one more entry on the node the disc goes off from, made only when the disc goes off, carrying damage and aspect riders but no terrain rider. The centre landing also carries the own damage, as a reached node's copy does (tentative, not the owner's: the doc says only "one more payload").
- The Heat ride row is an existing `AspectIntake` row whose identity is Fire (the concept the forge budgets) and whose `plants` names Heat's `StatusDef` (the field #{defs} adds). Landing it writes `t` through #{terrain}'s one writer. No new use class.
- **Rekeying `Infusion.load_of` / `use_of`** (this unit). Today `use_of` (`attack/spell/infusion.gd:119`) and `bag` (`:141`) look a concept's status up with `StatusRoster.shared().by_concept(id)`, which finds nothing for `fire`, so a Fire row would plant no rider. `load_of` keeps its concept keys (a Fire row's load is keyed `fire`, as the forge budget is). Every status lookup in `infusion.gd` goes through one resolver instead: the listed row's `AspectIntake.status()` (its `plants` when set), else the concept's only status, which is `by_concept(id)` for an aspect as today and the element's `terrain` for an element id (through #{defs}'s `ElementRoster`). `AspectIntake.status()`'s own fallback uses the same resolver, so an element row with `plants` unset plants its element's terrain. The forge's add / replace inside `load_of` is #{forge}'s; the forge readout's own lookup (`ui/hud/command_tray/bodies/readout_lines.gd:113`) is #{forge-ui}'s and is untouched here.

**Acceptance**
1. `test/unit/elements/test_burning_go_off.gd` (red first): a hit on a node with Heat > T goes off as a disc. The radius follows r = 2 × (√excess + 1) × node radius: with node radius R, excess 1 reaches a node at 3.9R and not one at 4.1R, and excess 4 reaches one at 5.9R and not one at 6.1R (concrete positions in a fixture; the test reads the knobs A and B, never the literals). The disc's nodes reached exclude the centre (its landing there is the gather's whole-hit entry, item 3b). Reach is read from a node's actual distance, allocated or not. The disc spends 1 Heat per node reached and may leave Heat under T.
2. Same file: a disc reaching no other node spends nothing and lands no copy and no centre landing (the hit node takes 1). A hit whose Heat, after its rider lands, is still ≤ T (excess 0) lands plainly (no copy, no spend). A hit whose Heat rider lifts the node from T to T+1 or more sets it off in that same hit (riders first, ADR 0083 §1).
3. Same file: power and primed-ness are both read after the hit's Heat rider lands (a node at T goes off on the hit that tips it, not the next), so repeated 1-Heat hits on a sub-reach node grow the disc until it first catches a neighbour. Each copy carries the payload at `share` (damage × share, riders ceil(r × share); at the default 1, the whole payload) plus the own damage, `damage_per_power × power`: the disc's fourth return under #{resolver}'s `GoOff` contract.
3b. Same file, the whole hit (`docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, "Every landing is a whole hit"): the hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it; a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2; terrain riders (Heat / Cold / Charge) land once per node per hit, so the extra landings carry damage and aspect riders only. In this unit's numbers, at the default share 1: a hit carrying damage D, a Poison rider p and a Heat rider h onto a Burning node C whose disc reaches one other node N lands 2 landings on C (each with damage D and Poison p) and 1 on N (D plus the own damage, Poison p, Heat h). Heat lands once on the centre: C's Heat moves by h − 1 (its rider, then the spend for N), never by 2h − 1. The node both reach is item 5. Assert in a separate test that C's centre landing also carries the own damage, so that test alone moves if the owner rules otherwise (tentative, not the owner's).
4. Chains: a copy's Heat rider lands on the reached node, and a reached Burning node goes off in turn, once per node per hit; it takes its copy plus its own centre landing (2 landings, its copy's Heat once).
5. Dual reach: a node holding both Explosive charges and Burning sets off both discs in one hit, and a node inside both takes two entries (the node both reach takes 2), each with every rider and its damage. The two reaches are concatenated; #{resolver}'s dedupe switch off keeps both entries, on collapses them to one (ADR 0084 §5). On the hit node, Burning's reach adds exactly one centre entry; the hit node's total is 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it, with go-off zero's disc counted as #{go-off-zero} answers for its own centre (1 + 2 if it lands there too). Assert entry counts here, not the Explosive disc's amounts.
5b. Radius seam: a test calls `disc_radius` for power 1 and 4 and asserts 4R and 6R at the default knobs; changing A or B in the def moves Burning's disc and the function's value together. The light and the Fire shape are tested in {fire} and {cast-shapes}, but both call this function.
6. Ride row: a spell with a Heat ride row (identity Fire, `plants` Heat) writes Heat into the landed node's `t` and plants no status row. It depends on 6b.
6b. Rekeying, `test/unit/spell/test_infusion.gd` (red first): a spell whose one intake row has identity Fire, `plants` Heat and innate n gives `Infusion.innate(spell).bag(spell).riders` exactly one `ApplyStatusEffect` whose status is Heat at power n (today it gives none: `by_concept(&"fire")` is null); the same row with `plants` unset gives the same rider (Fire's terrain); `load_of(spell)` keys that load `fire`; `Infusion.use_of(spell, &"fire")` returns a `PlantUse`. A Poison row with `plants` unset still resolves Poison by concept, and the existing asserts in `test_infusion.gd` and `test/unit/spell/test_aspect_intake.gd` pass unchanged.
7. Go-off zero's characterization tests (the files #{go-off-zero} leaves green) pass un-re-pointed, so this unit changes no Explosive behaviour.
8. Visible (this unit's look): a Burning disc plays on the blast VFX in Fire's colour at its reached radius, readable as different from go-off zero's, picked by the `GoOff` the mark names (#{resolver}'s seat). Check it in the sandbox host's blast tab; `mise run check` only.
