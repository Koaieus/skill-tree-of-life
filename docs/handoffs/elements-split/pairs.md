The pair rules of the #1716 split (design home `docs/design/elements.md` § Pair rules): what needs two elements at once and so cannot sit in Fire, Lightning or Ice while those run in parallel — Frozen muting a node's own go-offs until the break releases them, Frozen ending an arc or a disc's copy, dual reach across two elements (read through #{resolver}'s dedupe switch), Catalyst under a Frozen node, and the cross-element pins, each with its landing count under the whole-hit rule. Mostly pins on what the three element units built, plus one branch.
Blocked by: #{fire}, #{lightning}, #{ice}, #{terrain-ui}
Blocks: #{cast-shapes}

## Acceptance spec

**Decisions**
- Pair rules are their own unit after Fire / Lightning / Ice: *"option 1. or whichever fits a DAG."* (owner, 2026-11-10).
- **Frozen mutes the node's own go-offs; the break releases what it trapped** (owner, 2026-11-08: *ice wins — frozen solid*, then *trapped until broken*; `docs/design/elements.md:834-846`; ADR 0081 §4; ADR 0083 §2): a node both Frozen and Shocked neither receives nor emits; the hit that breaks the ice sets off the Shocked state in the same settle, plus the shatter burst. A hit whose Heat rider thaws the node is read against the thawed band: no longer Frozen, nothing muted, no shatter. At a Frozen node an arriving copy is admitted or absorbed as the node stands (an absorbed copy lands no riders); the hit's element riders then land, and primed-ness, power, the Frozen path-end and the break check are all read from the result (ADR 0083 §1, §2). The breaking hit's crit and damage are its own facts, fixed before it lands (ADR 0083 §4). The released arc reads its power **after the breaking hit lands** (owner, 2026-11-10; the spend law, `docs/design/elements.md:93-96`), so a breaker carrying Charge feeds the arc it releases.
- **Frozen ends a path**: *"a path **ends**: hops, chains and arcs stop here"* (owner, 2026-11-06; l.884-893); a copy under the break threshold is absorbed whole, a breaking copy lands, a direct hit lands (owner, 2026-11-09; l.77, ADR 0082 §3).
- **Dual reach** concatenates: *"the double counting might be a feature! tentative option 2, where union is kept as an easily switched to option if we change our minds. after all it's just a "do you dedupe the list or not" after gathering each reach medium's additional targets"* (owner, 2026-11-05; l.770-784) — every primed state present at read fires, each spending its own stacks; the dedupe is one switch at the gather step, `dedupe_dual_reach`, which is #{resolver}'s (Knobs). Then, on a node gathered by two reaches: *"riders twice, payload twice. charged burning nodes sounds like a dangerous concept"* (owner, 2026-11-10): two gathered entries, each landing its aspect riders and its damage at its share (default 1). Terrain riders (Heat / Cold / Charge) are stage one's and land once per node per hit (`docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, stage one's once-per-node-per-hit cap, restated in "Every landing is a whole hit"), so the second entry lands no Heat, Cold or Charge. On the same call: the node's own go-off fires once per hit (owner, 2026-11-10, picked over once per gathered entry), through the once-per-node set. The same switch covers a cast's shape against another element's go-off (owner, 2026-11-10, picked *same as dual reach: concatenate*; ADR 0084 §5); that side is #{cast-shapes}'s, built and tested there.
- **Every landing is a whole hit, and a go-off lands on its own node too** (owner, 2026-11-10, night, picked over share ½ with the centre excluded and over share 1 with the centre excluded: "the hit hits *and* the blast hits, and the arc hits, and share 1 means no rounding for rider stacks"; `docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, the paragraph of that name; no ADR yet — it changes ADR 0082's share default, now 1, and ADR 0084 §5's "the seed node takes the seed once"). The hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it; a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2; terrain riders land once per node per hit, so the extra landings carry damage and aspect riders only. The doc keeps "No other node reached, no go-off": the centre landing happens only when the go-off does. For the pairs that gives: a node both Burning and Shocked, hit, takes 1 + 2 = 3; under the mute the node's go-off does not go off, so there is no centre landing (a Frozen + Shocked node hit under the threshold takes 1); the break releases the arc, which goes off and lands its centre payload (the breaking hit's node takes 2, plus the shatter burst). The shatter adds its burst and no landing; tuning it to match the doubling is open (NOTES). A cast's shape alone is not a channel (owner, 2026-11-10: *"the shape for spells hmmm i think that one shouldn't count"*); that side is #{cast-shapes}'s.
- Catalyst and Cold coexist: *"Catalyst under a Frozen node is colourless until a hit's element lands; a direct Cold hit deepens the freeze (a Cold copy is absorbed), a Heat hit thaws harder"* (l.996-1001) — reviewed, taken as settled.
- *"Heat & Cold don't make sense together. frostBITE is an ailment"* (owner, 2026-11-04; l.998-1000) — Heat never touches a Frostbite pack; carried by #1444, not here (NOTES).

**Knobs** — none owned here. The tests read three that other units own, each set explicitly, never assumed:
- `dedupe_dual_reach` (bool, `false` = concatenate, the owner's tentative option 2): #{resolver} owns the switch, its surface and its test at both settings; it is also the shape-vs-go-off switch (ADR 0084 §5). This unit keeps only the pair-rule tests that read it (acceptance 6).
- Each go-off's `share`, default 1 since the whole-hit call (#{resolver}): the tests run at the default, where the round-up per rider is idle.
- The Frozen band's break threshold and shatter knobs (#{ice}): the tests read the burst through them, never a literal.

**Composition**
- Burning and Frozen cannot coexist on a node: Heat and Cold net into one signed `t` (#{terrain}). So "Frozen mutes the node's own go-offs" is Frozen + Shocked in practice; build no Frozen + Burning branch.
- **The mute** is one branch at #{resolver}'s post-rider read, where Ice's path-end and break answer (#{ice}) is asked: when the node's temperature band is Frozen and that answer says this hit does not break it, the node's other bands' go-offs are dropped from the read. The read is taken after the hit's element riders land (ADR 0083 §1, §2), so a Heat rider that thaws the node lifts the mute for the same hit, and a Cold rider that freezes it mutes for the same hit. A dropped go-off does not go off: it spends nothing, reaches nothing and lands no centre payload. When the hit breaks the ice, the shatter (#{ice}) and the released Shocked go-off (#{lightning}) commit in the same generation, each spending its own stacks; the arc's power is the Charge after the breaking hit's riders land (owner, 2026-11-10); the released arc goes off like any go-off, so it lands its centre payload on the node. If the gather's shape makes this more than one branch reading two existing answers, stop and say so (a fork, not a refactor).
- **Frozen ending an arc / a disc copy** needs no new code: Ice's `admits` on the Frozen band answers every arriving copy as it arrives, whoever's go-off sent it, and an absorbed copy lands no riders (ADR 0083 §2). This unit pins it across elements. A go-off still pays for a Frozen node it reached: its spend is read before the next generation is admitted. With #{lightning}'s transfer default, the Charge stack an arc sends to a Frozen neighbour whose copy is absorbed is absorbed with it, so that stack is voided (tentative, not the owner's: the doc treats a transferred stack exactly like the Charge rider an arc copy lands, and an absorbed copy lands no riders).
- **Dual reach**: each go-off gathers its own list; #{resolver}'s gather concatenates them, or dedupes when its switch is on. A node reached twice is two entries (owner, 2026-11-10); each entry lands its aspect riders and its damage at its share, so the node takes those twice and has two rows in the blast table, while its terrain riders land once (stage one's cap). Its own go-off fires once per hit (owner, 2026-11-10). No new code: this unit pins the composition across Fire and Lightning, with the whole-hit counts.
- Owns nothing new beyond the mute branch. Readers: the record's blast table, the blast VFX path.

**Acceptance** (all in `test/unit/attack/test_element_pairs.gd`; exact `CombatWorld` fixtures. Terrain is set by a direct write through `NodeCombat.add_terrain` (#{terrain}). Hits are test-built direct hits — no arrow, no spell — carrying damage D, a Poison rider of p and, where named, one element rider (`effects/status/{heat,cold,charge}.tres`, #{defs}), and the copies their go-offs send. Every counted node is enemy-held; `crit_chance` is zeroed except where a crit is the breaker; every go-off runs at the default `share` 1; Burning's own-damage knob (#{burning}'s `damage_per_power`) is zeroed. A node *takes n* when n landings reach it in the hit: it gains n × p Poison stacks and is dealt n × D by landings, a shatter burst counted apart.)
1. Frozen + Shocked, a direct hit under the break threshold: the node takes 1 (the muted arc does not go off, so no centre landing); no arc; no Charge spent; neighbours untouched.
2. Frozen + Shocked with three unfrozen neighbours, a breaking direct hit (the break check read after its riders land): damage ≥ the ice break threshold, and separately a crit under it. In both the shatter burst lands, the node arcs to every neighbour in the same settle, and both spends commit. In the damage run the node takes 2 (the hit and the released arc's centre landing) plus the burst, and each neighbour takes 1. Run it twice more: with a breaker carrying no Charge rider the arc's power is the trapped Charge's excess; with a breaker carrying Charge the power includes what it landed (post-land), and on a node sitting one stack under the next ladder level that rider lifts the arc a level.
2b. Frozen + Shocked, a direct hit under the break threshold carrying a Heat rider big enough to thaw the node: the band is read after the rider, so the node is not Frozen; no shatter, and the Shocked go-off fires normally: the node takes 2, each neighbour 1, and the node's `t` rises by exactly the rider (once, not per landing).
3. Frozen + Shocked, reached by a copy from a Burning node's disc (at share 1 a copy is under the threshold when its hit is): an under-threshold copy is absorbed whole — the node takes 0, its riders land nothing (Cold and Charge unchanged), no arc. A breaking copy lands and releases the arc: the node takes 2 (the copy and its arc's centre landing) plus the burst.
4. A Shocked node's arc whose neighbour is Frozen (not Shocked): that neighbour absorbs the under-threshold copy (takes 0) and no hop continues past it; the other neighbours take 1 and the Shocked node takes 2. The arc still pays 1 Charge for the Frozen neighbour, and with #{lightning}'s transfer default that neighbour's Charge is unchanged (Composition; tentative). Same for a Burning disc's copy onto a Frozen node: absorbed, and the disc still spends 1 Heat for it. A Frozen node inside both the disc and the arc of one Burning + Shocked node takes 0 under a hit below the threshold; under a hit at or above it both copies land (it takes 2) and the ice shatters once (one burst, one spend).
5. A Cold rider (no arrow): a direct hit carrying Cold c ≤ T onto a Shocked node at `t` = 0 lays c Cold on every arc target (`ceil(c × share)`, c at share 1) and c on the hit node once, not once per landing; onto a Burning node whose excess stays ≥ 1 after the Cold nets, it lays c Cold over the disc, and the node's Heat drops by c once plus the disc's spend (l.512-516). In the same hit (riders first, ADR 0083 §1): a Cold rider that nets the Burning node's Heat to T or below puts it out — no disc, no spend, the node takes 1; a Cold rider on a hit under the break threshold that takes a Shocked node's Cold count past T freezes it — the arc is muted, no Charge spent, the node takes 1.
6. The whole-hit arithmetic across two elements (`docs/design/elements.md` § Landing resolution, "Every landing is a whole hit"): the hit node takes 1 + 1 per channel that goes off from it; a node one channel reaches takes 1, a node both reach takes 2; terrain riders (Heat / Cold / Charge) land once per node per hit, so the extra landings carry damage and aspect riders only. Fixture: a node both Burning and Shocked (level 1), hit once by a direct hit carrying +1 Heat, with three bystanders — a neighbour inside the disc (both reach), a neighbour outside it (arc only), a non-neighbour inside it (disc only). Both go off, each spending its own stacks. With `dedupe_dual_reach = false`: the hit node takes 3; the both-reach neighbour 2, with two blast-table rows; each one-reach bystander 1. The hit node's Heat ends at pre-hit + 1 − the disc's spend (the rider lands once, not three times), and every bystander's Heat rises by exactly 1, the both-reach neighbour's included. With `true`: the both-reach neighbour takes 1, with one row, and its Heat still rises by 1; the one-reach bystanders are unchanged. The hit node's count under `true` is not asserted here (NOTES).
6b. Same fixture, the both-reach neighbour also made Burning (the drone places it so its own disc reaches only one fresh enemy node), switch `false`: its disc fires once per hit, not once per entry — it takes 3 (two entries and its own disc's centre landing) and spends Heat once, for the one node its disc reaches, which takes 1.
7. Catalyst under Frozen: 3 Catalyst + a Heat hit of 1 converts to 4 Heat netted against the Cold (a bigger thaw than the Heat alone); a direct Cold hit converts into Cold and deepens; a Cold copy is absorbed and leaves the Catalyst untouched.
8. Visual, the trapped Charge (handed over from #{terrain-ui}, which already draws both bars on a Frozen node): the player can tell that the Charge on a Frozen + Shocked node is trapped. It is held under the ice and will go off when the ice breaks, which looks different from Charge on an unfrozen node. The read comes from the band function alone, on the node's bars and tooltip; no stored flag.
9. Visual: a release beat shows the shatter and the arcs on the same beat, played from the record; a dual reach of one Burning + Shocked node draws both marks (disc and arcs) at that node. Checked in the blast sandbox tab, screenshot attached to the report.
10. Every surviving test of #{fire}, #{lightning}, #{ice} stays green unedited.
