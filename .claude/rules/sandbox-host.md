The in-editor **sandbox host** (`addons/sandbox_host/`) unifies the dev-tooling surfaces as tabs; a live tab is an **inherited scene** of `sandbox_live_tab.tscn` that *instances its panel scene inside itself* under `%PanelHost` (copy `tabs/70_bloom_tab.tscn`) — never code-composed, generated, or injected via the legacy `panel_scene` export, which `add_child`s at runtime and silently breaks SubViewport post-processing in a dock.
