About to hardcode a tuning rate, bolt an extra bucket onto a pool, or assign `pool.base_value` directly? Each has a house answer: the rate is an ordinary board stat read as a formula input (never a genesis param or a plain var), the bucket is a clamping-setter bin with named transfer methods, and a raw `base_value` write deliberately skips the cap ratchet — use `PoolStat.set_base_ratcheted` unless you mean to skip it. See docs/domain/stat-knobs-and-bins.md
