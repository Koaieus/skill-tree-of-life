About to hardcode a tuning rate, bolt an extra bucket onto a pool, or assign `pool.base_value` directly? Each has a house answer — an ordinary board stat read as a formula input, a clamping-setter bin with named transfer methods, and one door onto a cap whose rise/fall policy (and, since #660, whose stored representation) is authored on the def, never chosen by the caller.
