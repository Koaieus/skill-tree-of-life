About to hardcode a tuning rate, bolt an extra bucket onto a pool, or write `base_value` to force a stat to an exact number? Each has a house answer — a board stat read as a formula input, a clamping-setter bin with named transfer methods, and one door onto a *pool's cap*, whose rise/fall policy and stored representation are authored on the def and never chosen by the caller — while a **derived** stat, whose base is only one input, wants a `SET` modifier, and a content resource's **own** number (a spell's range) is an overlay bin for `get_local_value_with`, never a stat's base. See docs/domain/stat-knobs-and-bins.md
