About to hardcode a tuning rate, bolt an extra bucket onto a pool, or assign `pool.base_value` directly? Each has a house answer — an ordinary board stat read as a formula input, a clamping-setter bin with named transfer methods, and `PoolStat.set_base_ratcheted` (a raw `base_value` write deliberately skips the cap ratchet).
