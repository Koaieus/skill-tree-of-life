Prefer a `.tscn` you `instantiate()` over a code-composed `X.new()` + `add_child` tree, and inject cross-system deps as `@export` NodePaths wired by the composing scene rather than `get_node` in code — a scene must ship pre-packaged with the children its `@onready`s depend on, and a base scene that packages no children is dead weight — and such an export resolves *before* its target's `_ready`, so a subscription made in its setter can silently bind a resource the target then replaces.
