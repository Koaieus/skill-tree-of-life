Prefer a `.tscn` you `instantiate()` over a code-composed `X.new()` + `add_child` tree, and inject cross-system deps as `@export` NodePaths wired by the composing scene rather than `get_node` in code — a scene ships pre-packaged with the children its `@onready`s depend on, and such an export resolves *before* its target's `_ready`. See docs/domain/scene-composition.md An INSTANCE carries two export-only traps, right from source and wrong only in a build: below another instance's root it needs `[editable path=…]` (#711), and re-anchoring needs an authored `anchors_preset`; `mise run check` lints both.
