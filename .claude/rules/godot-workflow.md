An editor pass can re-serialize the scenes and `.tres` files it touches, but the damage is bounded, always visible in `git diff`, and usually just default-elision or id noise — refresh freely, diff after, restore the rare non-default value that vanished, move on. Don't stall, avoid the refresh, or spend turns investigating it. Also: a stale `class_name` cache breaks parsing until you refresh, a deferred call holding a freed Object is dropped, and `.uid` sidecars orphan on every move or delete.
