Run `mise run refresh` for a headless editor refresh — it reports a verdict (`nothing changed`, or just the non-trivial lines removed) so you never hand-diff or judge editor churn yourself. The damage is bounded and git-visible; refresh freely, read the one-line verdict, move on — don't stall, avoid the refresh, or investigate. Also: a stale `class_name` cache breaks parsing until you refresh, a deferred call holding a freed Object is dropped, and `.uid` sidecars orphan on every move or delete.
