Run `mise run refresh` for a headless editor refresh — it reports a verdict (`nothing changed`, or just the non-trivial lines removed) so you never hand-diff or judge editor churn yourself. The damage is bounded and git-visible; refresh freely, read the one-line verdict, move on — don't stall, avoid the refresh, or investigate. Two more symptoms route here: *"Could not find type X"* on a `class_name` you just added or renamed (stale class cache — refresh), and a tracked `.uid` pointing at nothing after you move or delete a `.gd` (sweep for orphans).
