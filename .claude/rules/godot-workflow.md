An editor pass round-trips scenes and can silently strip `.tres` fields, so always `git diff` after one; a stale `class_name` cache breaks parsing until `godot --headless --editor --quit` rebuilds it; a deferred call holding a freed Object is dropped with no error; and `.uid` sidecars orphan on every move or delete. File-format gotchas load on their own when you open a `.tscn`, `.tres` or `.gdshader`. See docs/domain/godot-workflow.md
