## Other axes in free-text answers

- **Perf-first**: "how many nodes / how fast" before committing (binning,
  physics gather, blade C++ port) — perf, not cleanliness, is often the real
  tiebreaker.
- **Settle now vs park** comes up constantly; correlates with clean-vs-cheap
  but is not it — sometimes "settle now" is the cheap move.
- **Redirect the premise**: the owner often picks no listed option and
  questions the framing or offers a new synthesis (landed in `unclear`).
  Options are a prompt to think aloud, not a strict menu — a scorecard must
  survive an "Other" answer.
- Scope / balance / naming forks vastly outnumber clean-vs-cheap ones.
